## Section 1 — Signing in

The site is at **abfresources.com**. It's a private leader hub — there's a password gate on the front page so only ABF leaders and teachers get in. Sign in with the leader password (Tom shares it with leaders directly).

Once you're inside, you land on a single-page hub. There's a **role prompt in the top right** — click your name to identify yourself as an ABF leader. That tells the site to show you the leader views (the Teacher Availability grid, New Request form, My Requests tab).

That's it. No accounts, no passwords to manage, no email confirmations. One sign-in, and the rest of the site knows who you are.

## Section 3 — Teacher Scheduling (the main event for leaders)

**The big idea:** No more chasing teachers down by text or email to find someone for an upcoming Sunday. The site shows you a single grid of who's available when. You pick a teacher, send a request through the site, and move on with your week.

### The Teacher Availability grid

When you sign in as a leader, you see the **Teacher Availability** grid:
- **Rows are teachers** — every teacher on the LEFC roster
- **Columns are Sundays** — every Sunday from September through June
- Cell colors: **green = available**, **gold = tentative**, **red = unavailable**, **blue = already booked** by another ABF leader
- **Diagonal-stripe columns** are no-class Sundays (Family Worship, Easter, summer break) — you can ignore those

In one glance, you can see who's open for the dates you need. Click any teacher's name to pull up their profile — bio, training, the courses they have ready to teach, and how many weeks each course typically runs.

### Sending a teaching request

When you find a teacher you want, click **New Request**. You'll fill out a short form:
- **Teacher** — who you're asking
- **Course** *(optional)* — filtered to courses that teacher already offers. Leave blank to let them pick. Choose "Other" if you have a custom topic in mind.
- **Sundays you'd like them** — click as many as apply. Sundays the teacher already flagged as unavailable are marked, but you can still send the request anyway in case their plans changed.
- **Message to the teacher** — a short personal note. Why this series, why them, why these dates.

Hit **Send request**. The teacher gets it in their Incoming Requests tab and accepts or declines. You'll see the response when it comes back.

### Tracking what you've sent

Your **My Requests** tab keeps every request you've ever sent in one list — pending, accepted, declined. No more scrolling through old text threads to remember what's been confirmed. It's all here.

### Why this matters

You're a lay volunteer with limited margin. The old workflow — text a teacher, wait, follow up, lose the thread, ask again, hope someone says yes by Friday — burned hours every quarter. This tool turns it into a single screen and a few clicks. Less administrative weight. More margin for the actual ministry — caring for your people, leading discussion, praying together.

## Section 4 — The other features (quick lap)

Even though the focus today is signing in and scheduling, here's what else is on the site so you know it's there:

- **ABF Classes directory** — a quick reference of every active ABF, who leads it, and which service.
- **Key Dates** — the calendar of no-meeting Sundays and ABF resume dates so you're never guessing whether your class meets this week.
- **Teacher roster** — the same teachers from the availability grid, organized by stage (Veterans, Active, Up & Comers in Years 2–3 and Year 1) — so you can see who's seasoned and who's still developing.
- **Resources** — articles on ABF leadership, training videos for new and existing leaders, and full Bible study series with embedded video sessions.
- **Class Feedback** — when you're ready, leaders can launch a feedback survey from a template, share a 5-digit code with their class, and review responses. (Worth its own walkthrough later.)
