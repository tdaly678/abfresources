Planning Meeting Retrospective — 1, 2, 3 John

Several months back a group of us spent an evening prepping the 1–3 John sermon series — surfacing themes, theological and cultural landmines, LEFC Rock connections, overlapping concepts, and sermon imagery.

The aim of the evening was to give the preaching pastors a richer pool to draw from, and to be a meaningful time of study for those of you contributing. We're surveying to make the next gathering more useful in equipping the church and more personally edifying for those involved.
