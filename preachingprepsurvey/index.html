Hebrews Kickoff

Beginning in September, we'll be preaching through the Epistle to the Hebrews — one of the most glorious and weighty books in Scripture. We'd like to invite you to a planning evening to dive into the text together, as we have for past series.

This quick survey helps us land on a date that works for everyone. And if you've joined us for a previous planning gathering, we'd love your honest feedback so we can keep sharpening this time — making it more edifying for the group and more useful for equipping the church.
