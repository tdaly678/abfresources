About ABF Ministry

Adult Bible Fellowships at LEFC are Sunday morning mid-size gatherings that come together around God's Word — studying Scripture, building community, and caring for one another. ABFs are a cornerstone of how LEFC makes and matures disciples. As a primarily lay-led ministry, ABF leaders are trusted to shepherd their groups with consistency and heart.

This resource hub exists to encourage leaders in the work they're doing, educate them in the values and vision of the ministry, equip them with practical tools and training, and empower them to lead autonomously and serve without issues — supported by pastoral staff, but never dependent on it.

Intro

22 min

Why doctrine matters for ordinary Christians, how the EFCA Statement of Faith is put together, and how to use its ten articles as a map of the gospel.

The Bible

33 min

God has spoken. What it means to call Scripture the verbally inspired Word of God, without error in the original writings — and why that makes it the final authority, to be believed in all it teaches, obeyed in all it requires, and trusted in all it promises.

God

33 min

One God, holy and infinitely perfect, eternally existing in a loving unity of three equally divine Persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — who purposed from eternity to redeem a people for Himself.

The Human Condition

35 min

Created in God’s image, fallen in Adam. Why human beings are sinners by nature and by choice, alienated from God and under His wrath, and why rescue has to come from outside us.

Jesus Christ

50 min

God incarnate — fully God and fully man, one Person in two natures. The virgin birth, the sinless life, the crucifixion under Pilate, the bodily resurrection and ascension, and Christ’s present work as our High Priest and Advocate.

The Work of Christ

41 min

The cross as substitution: Christ shed His blood as the perfect, all-sufficient sacrifice for our sins. Why His atoning death and victorious resurrection are the only ground for salvation.

The Holy Spirit

54 min

The Spirit convicts, regenerates, indwells, illuminates, guides, equips, and empowers believers for Christ-like living and service — and in all that He does, He glorifies the Lord Jesus Christ.

The Body of Christ

48 min

The true church — all who have been justified by grace through faith alone in Christ alone, united by the Spirit under Christ as Head. How that church is made visible in local congregations, and what baptism and the Lord’s Supper confirm and nourish.

Christian Living

51 min

God’s justifying grace is never separated from His sanctifying power. Loving God supremely and others sacrificially, compassion toward the poor and justice for the oppressed, combatting spiritual evil, and making disciples in word and deed.

Christ’s Return

32 min

The personal, bodily, and glorious return of the Lord Jesus at a time known only to God — our blessed hope, and why constant expectancy fuels godly living, sacrificial service, and energetic mission.

Response and Eternal Destiny

37 min

God commands everyone everywhere to turn to Him in repentance and receive the Lord Jesus Christ. The bodily resurrection of the dead, the final judgment, and the two eternal destinies that hang on that response.

Q&A Panel

1 hr 7 min

The full panel from Sunday, August 30 — the teaching team works through questions the class submitted across all ten articles of the Statement of Faith.
